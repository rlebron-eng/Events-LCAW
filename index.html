Announce MSCI ratings on iCR

Use LCAW as the launch platform to establish iCR as the first carbon registry with MSCI institutional ratings as standard. Position Centigrade as the horizontal integrity layer. Convert credibility into buyer demand and partner confidence.

Raise Centigrade and iCR visibility across VCM, CDR, climate risk, and digital infrastructure communities. Be known before people need us.
